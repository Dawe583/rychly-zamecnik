**Perex:** A jammed key, a slammed door or a lock that refuses to cooperate. Before you panic or resort to force, go through a few tips on how to proceed sensibly and safely.

## Tip 1: Don't force the key

If the key is catching in the lock, don't force it, don't try to „break it free“ and don't turn it violently.

A snapped key in the lock means a more complicated opening and often a replacement of the whole lock. Sometimes a gentle wiggle of the key or a light push of the door towards the frame is all it takes.

## Tip 2: Check whether the door has merely slammed shut

In many cases the lock isn't locked at all — the door has simply slammed shut. That's good news: such a door can often be opened without any damage.

- ✓ The door isn't locked with a key.
- ✓ The key isn't broken.
- ✓ The lock isn't damaged.

If all of the above holds, a professional opening is a matter of minutes.

## Tip 3: Avoid the „guaranteed tricks“ from the internet

A credit card, a screwdriver, a wire or a coat hanger? The tutorials look simple, but in practice they tend to end like this:

- ✗ a damaged door
- ✗ a ruined lock
- ✗ higher repair costs

What is meant to be a quick fix often turns into an expensive problem.

## When to call a locksmith

- The key won't turn.
- The lock is damaged.
- The key has snapped.
- You don't want to risk the damage.

In these cases an emergency locksmith is the best choice. An experienced 24/7 locksmith will open the lock gently, without needless damage and at a price agreed in advance.
