**Perex:** A slammed door or lost keys are stressful. And that stress is exactly what dishonest locksmiths target. We'll show you the most common tricks and, above all, how to recognise a fair 24/7 locksmith.

## 1. A suspiciously low price in the ad

„Door opening from CZK 499“ sounds tempting. The reality? On site the price easily climbs to CZK 5,000–10,000.

How they do it: the low price is only „for turning up“ and every further step is billed separately, and steeply.

- ✓ Always ask for the final indicative price on the phone.
- ✓ A serious locksmith service does not hide the price.

## 2. Drilling the lock straight away

An honest locksmith always tries to open without damage. A dishonest one often doesn't even attempt the gentle methods and drills immediately — which means an expensive lock replacement on top.

- ✓ Ask outright: „Can it be opened without damage?“
- ✓ If you hear „there's no other way“ the moment they arrive, be on your guard.

## 3. Pressure for an unnecessary lock replacement

> „That lock is dangerous, we have to replace it right away.“

A common trick, especially at night. In reality the lock is usually working fine and no replacement is needed.

- ✓ Ask for an explanation and a specific reason.
- ✓ You have every right to refuse the replacement.

## 4. Unclear identity and no receipt

The typical scenario: no company ID, no company, no proof of payment. You then have nowhere to complain and the service tends to be wildly overpriced.

- ✓ Choose an established locksmith business.
- ✓ A receipt is a matter of course, not a favour.

## 5. Fake „local firms“

The website claims „Prague 1“, the reality is a call centre outside Prague, a long journey and high travel charges.

- ✓ Check whether the firm really operates in Prague.
- ✓ Read the reviews — not just five-star ones with no text.

## How to recognise an honest locksmith

- ✓ They give you the price in advance, on the phone.
- ✓ They arrive within a reasonable time.
- ✓ They try to open without damage.
- ✓ They don't push unnecessary repairs.
- ✓ They issue a receipt.

## In short

- ✗ Extremely cheap offers are a risk, not a bargain.

- ✓ Insist on knowing the price before they set off.
- ✓ An honest 24/7 locksmith has nothing to hide.
