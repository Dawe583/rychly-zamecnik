**Perex:** Not every safe call-out is a drama. Sometimes it's more of a comedy — especially when it plays out in the middle of a busy department store.

## Locked at the worst possible moment

Early evening, a full shop and a safe holding the day's takings that refused to open. The electronic lock was reporting an error, the staff were trying the code for the sixth time and the queue at the tills kept growing.

## First rule: don't keep trying forever

Most electronic safe locks have protection against repeated entry. After several wrong attempts the lock blocks itself for a few minutes — and with every further attempt the delay gets longer.

- ✗ Trying the code over and over only makes things worse.

- ✓ Stop, wait, and call someone who can open the safe properly.

## How the job went

It turned out the problem wasn't the code but a flat battery — the lock had enough power to light up the display, but not to release the bolt. An external power supply, and the safe was open in moments.

## What to do when a safe won't open

- Try replacing the batteries — it's the most common cause.
- Don't enter the code repeatedly, you risk locking it out.
- Don't try to lever or drill it, you'll lose the safe's certification.
- Call a locksmith with experience of safes.
