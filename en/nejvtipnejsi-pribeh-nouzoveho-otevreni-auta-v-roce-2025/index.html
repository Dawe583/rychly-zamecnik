The funniest emergency car opening of 2025

One of the most curious situations of the year played out right in the heart of Prague and showed that an emergency car opening can be not only fast, but unexpectedly entertaining too.

A locked car, a tourist and a sausage in hand

It was shortly after noon, Wenceslas Square was pulsing with life, and among the tourists, trams and street performers there he was — a slightly bewildered foreign visitor. A camera in one hand, a freshly bought sausage in the other. The keys? They stayed inside the car, which had just locked itself with that characteristic click.

Despair gave way to some frantic googling: locksmith Prague 1 — emergency car opening.

A job that took minutes

The locksmith arrived within a few minutes. No smashed glass, no damage to the lock, just specialist tools and the calm of an experienced professional. As a small crowd began to gather around the car, the tourist remarked nervously:

&bdquo;If it doesn't work, at least we can share the sausage…&ldquo;

That was a mistake. The audience laughed so loudly that one of the street musicians spontaneously added a dramatic melody. At that very moment the locksmith opened the car — elegantly, quickly and without a single scratch. Applause. The sausage saved. The keys back in their owner's hand.

Why we remember that one

Because it captures exactly what an emergency car opening in Prague should look like:

  • a quick arrival, no waiting
  • opening the car without damage
  • professional and human at the same time
  • the situation solved with a smile

The takeaway

Whether you're a tourist or a local, a locked car doesn't discriminate. When it happens, don't panic, don't try to lever the door yourself, and call a locksmith who has experience with emergency car openings.
