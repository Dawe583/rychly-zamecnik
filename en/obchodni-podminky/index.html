Terms of service

So that it is clear where we stand from the start. What we agree on the phone holds — this document just writes it down.

Who provides the services

Services are provided by Rychlý Zámečník, company ID 075 25 711, e-mail info@rychly-zamecnik.cz, telephone +420 723 965 990 (the „contractor“).

What these terms cover

They apply to locksmith work carried out at the customer's location — emergency opening of doors, cars and safes, replacement and repair of locks and cylinders, door repairs and security work.

How an order is made

An order is placed by phone or e-mail. Before the call-out the contractor states an indicative price and an approximate arrival time. The contract is concluded the moment the customer confirms the call-out.

Prices shown on this website are indicative, in the „from“ format. The final price depends on the type of lock, the extent of the damage and the material used.

The price and its confirmation

  • The technician assesses the job on site and states the final price before any work begins.
  • Work starts only after the customer approves that price.
  • If it turns out during the job that more work is needed, the technician stops and agrees a new price.

The customer is not obliged to order the work if the stated price does not suit them. In that case only the call-out fee per the price list is payable.

Call-out fee and surcharges

A call-out fee per the price list is added to the price of the work. Surcharges for night hours, weekends and public holidays are listed in the price list and the technician points them out when confirming the price. Discounts for seniors and disability card holders apply on presentation of the document.

Proof of entitlement

Before a door, vehicle or safe is opened, the customer must prove they are entitled to have it opened — by an ID document showing the address, a tenancy agreement, a vehicle registration certificate or other credible means.

Without that proof the technician will not carry out the work. This is not a formality — it protects the owner as much as the contractor. The call-out fee remains payable in such a case.

Payment

Payment is due once the work is finished, in cash or by bank transfer. A receipt is issued for every job. On request the contractor will issue an invoice to a company or documentation for an insurer.

Warranty

The contractor provides a 24-month warranty on the work carried out and the material supplied, from the date of handover. The warranty does not cover defects caused by normal wear, forced damage, unqualified interference by a third party or improper use.

Complaints

Make a complaint by phone or e-mail, ideally with a description of the defect and a photograph. The contractor will settle it within 30 days at the latest unless a longer period is agreed with the customer.

If the complaint is justified, the contractor removes the defect free of charge. Where that is not possible, they provide a reasonable discount or refund the amount paid.

Withdrawal from the contract

For contracts concluded at a distance or off business premises, a consumer has the right to withdraw within 14 days. For an urgent repair the consumer has expressly requested, that right lapses on performance of the service under the Czech Civil Code — which is the typical case with emergency opening.

An order can be cancelled free of charge until the technician has set off. Once they are on the way, the call-out fee is payable.

Liability for damage

The contractor is liable for damage demonstrably caused while carrying out the work. With emergency opening, damage to the lock may be unavoidable — the technician points this out in advance and agrees both the procedure and the price of any replacement.

Dispute resolution

Disputes are settled primarily by agreement. A consumer has the right to out-of-court resolution before the Czech Trade Inspection Authority (adr.coi.cz).

Effective date

These terms are effective from 1 January 2026. Orders already placed are governed by the wording effective at the time of the order.

This document is prepared as a starting point, not as finished legal text. Before the website starts using it, have it checked by someone who can take responsibility for its wording — especially the periods, warranty and withdrawal.
